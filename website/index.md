YOU ARE IN CONTROL

### Start with automatic picks. Keep the final say.

Use the timeline and frame steps to find the moment you want. Smart Select keeps the frames you have selected and fills the remaining slots.

- See candidates on a video timeline

- Move by 1 or 5 frames at a time

- Selected frames follow the video’s timeline

MAKE EACH STORYBOARD YOURS

### The details you need, without a complicated workflow.

#### Grid size

Choose from 3 × 3 to 8 × 8, depending on how much detail you need.

#### Add a filename watermark

Choose to show the filename and timecodes so you can quickly find the right video later.

#### Match your video

Use the video's aspect ratio and choose a background color.

#### Organise videos in batches

Add several videos at once. Watch previews fill as frames are ready, or pause after the current video.

YOUR VIDEOS STAY YOURS

### Your videos stay on your Mac.

Frame extraction and image export happen on your Mac. Videos are never uploaded. The optional update check reads public GitHub release information.

BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

### What you may want to know.

#### Do I need an account to use ShotMap?

No. Download the app and start making storyboards on your Mac.

#### Which Macs are supported?

ShotMap supports macOS 13 or later. Choose the Apple Silicon (M-series) or Intel installer on the release page.

#### Can I replace a frame myself?

Yes. Smart Select is a starting point; the Manual Frame Editor lets you choose the final frames.

#### Where are storyboard images saved?

By default, beside the source video. Use Save As to choose another location.
