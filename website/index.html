PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your footage stays on your Mac.

Frame analysis and export happen locally. There is no account, upload, cloud queue, or telemetry to set up.

INSTALL AND START

Ready in a few simple steps.

Download the latest release, move ShotMap to Applications, and open it. No account or command line is required.

Requires macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Does ShotMap upload my videos?

No. Reading frames and creating the final image happen entirely on your Mac.

Which Macs are supported?

ShotMap supports macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Can I replace a frame myself?

Yes. Smart Select is a starting point; the manual frame picker lets you choose the moments yourself.
