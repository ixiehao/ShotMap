BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

What you may want to know.

Do I need an account to use ShotMap?

No. Download the app and start making storyboards on your Mac.

Which Macs are supported?

ShotMap supports macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Can I replace a frame myself?

Yes. Smart Select is a starting point; the Manual Frame Editor lets you choose the final frames.

Where are storyboard images saved?

By default, beside the source video. Use Save As to choose another location.
